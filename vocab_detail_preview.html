Hund
Plural: die Hunde
🇬🇧 dog
A1 ⚡ Rank #450
Meaning & Explanation
"Hund" is a masculine noun referring to a domestic dog (canis lupus familiaris) — the most popular pet in Germany. It's one of the first animals you'll learn at A1 level and is used the same way as "dog" in English.
💡 Usage: Neutral — works in both formal and informal contexts. Affectionately you'll often hear "Hundi". "Köter" is derogatory and should be avoided.
🐾 Fun Fact
Germany has one of the highest dog ownership rates in Europe — dogs are allowed in many restaurants, shops, and even on public transport. A real culture shock for many visitors! And yes, the "Hundesteuer" (dog tax) is a real thing — dog owners pay €50–150 per year per registered dog.
